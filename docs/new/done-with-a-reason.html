Done, with a reason - and a tidier look

The desk asked for two things after a day of real use, so they shipped the same evening.

  • Done is one click. If you want to say why - filed, forwarded, called them - tap a chip after. Optional, never a form.
  • Done for all of these: tick several faxes, close them together.
  • Junk now offers to block the sender in the same breath. Blocking stays on your PC and is reversible from the Spam view.
  • New colours (ledger blue and vermilion), a new tray icon, and the same single EXE - no installer.

Why a reason at all? Because three weeks later somebody asks "what happened to the Anthem fax?" and the answer is on the row: Done - forwarded - Maria - Oct 1.

How it works at the desk
  1. Open the fax, or don't - Done works from the list.
  2. Press Done. The row closes.
  3. Want to note why? Tap + why and pick a chip. That's it.

Free for one desk. One file, no installer, nothing leaves your PC but the fax.
