Road: the office faxes on your phone, and the field back to the office

The doctor is at the hospital. The fax with the referral is on the office PC. A signed consent is in the car. Road closes that gap without email, texting, or a cloud fax account.

Road is a page the phone opens - freefaxdesk.com/road.html - and a switch in FaxDesk Settings. The office invites a phone with an 8-character code; the phone makes its own key (it never leaves the phone, and we never see it); the office presses Allow and checks the phone's code so a stranger's phone cannot slip in. From then on the desk can send any fax or document to that phone, and the phone can photograph pages or pick a PDF and send them in - they land in the office inbox as one PDF, assigned to whoever was picked.

  • Sealed end to end. Each document is encrypted on the sending side to the receiving side's key (P-256, AES-GCM), and bound to this office and this phone. The relay (api.freefaxdesk.com) stores locked envelopes for up to 30 days and can read none of them - not the pages, not the names, not who it is for. The audit log on the PC records that something went to a phone, never what.
  • Nothing to install on the phone. Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android. Add it to the home screen for the app feel and a buzz when something arrives (the buzz carries no content - the phone fetches the real thing).
  • The office stays in charge. Settings > Road lists every phone with when it was last seen; Block or Remove works instantly. Turn Road off and the office key is gone.
  • Rate-limited relay, open code. The relay is a small Cloudflare Worker whose source is in the repo; an office can run its own by changing one setting.

Road ships in 1.3.1 together with a fix: 1.3.0 dropped documents from phones that had real photographs in them (the office side capped the page bundle too low). If you tried Road on 1.3.0 and a send never arrived, that was it.

How it works at the desk
  1. On the PC: Settings > Road > Turn Road on, then type whose phone it is and press Invite a phone. You get a code (good for 24 hours).
  2. On the phone: open freefaxdesk.com/road.html, type the code and your name, Join. Open This phone and read the office the first four characters of the code shown there.
  3. Back on the PC: press Allow, type those four characters. Done - To phone appears on every fax, and the phone's Send tab reaches the office.

Free for one desk. One file, no installer, nothing leaves your PC but the fax.
