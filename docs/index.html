FaxDesk is a small free program. Drop a PDF, it goes out with a cover sheet. Incoming faxes show up on the page, get assigned to a person and marked handled. Everything stays on your computer.

Send

Drop a PDF, pick the name from your address book, add a note. A cover sheet with your logo goes first. It queues and sends within a minute; you get a transmission report with the cover as sent.

Incoming

New faxes appear within minutes. Open, print, assign to a person, mark handled, name a sender, block spam, or set "always send this sender to Susie".

Log

Every fax sent or failed, with the reason in plain words, a printable confirmation, and search.

Private by design

No cloud account. No telemetry. Files live in a folder on your PC and are deleted after the number of days you choose. The only thing it talks to is api.phone.com.

How it works

Download and set up (5 minutes)

  1. Download FaxDesk.exe (Windows 10/11). Mac: download the zip and double-click FaxDesk.command (needs Python 3).
  2. Run it. Your browser opens on the setup page.
  3. Settings: paste your Phone.com API token (Phone.com > API), your account id and the extension of your fax line. Test connection.
  4. Add your people's names. Send a one-page test to yourself.

Questions

Do I need a Phone.com account? FaxDesk started on Phone.com and that is the tested path; other services are coming, and you can ask for yours. If you do not have a line yet, this link gets you a Phone.com fax number.

Is it secure? Your token is stored sealed to your Windows account. Faxes are files on your PC. FaxDesk never sends anything anywhere except to Phone.com to fax it.

Who made this? A small medical office that wanted its fax line to behave like email, built it for the front desk, and decided to give it away. The office stays anonymous; the code does not - it is open on GitHub under the MIT licence.
