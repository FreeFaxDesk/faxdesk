Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-27. Applies to the FaxDesk software and to freefaxdesk.com. Published by HnS, LLC, a Wyoming company.

The software (FaxDesk)

FaxDesk sends nothing to us. It has no account, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no update check and no analytics. The only network connection it makes is to the fax provider you configure (today, Phone.com), and only to send and receive your faxes and to list the accounts and numbers your own API token can see.

Your data stays on your PC. Received faxes, sent faxes, cover pages, confirmations, your address book, sender names, assignments and the audit log are files in a folder on the computer running FaxDesk (by default %LOCALAPPDATA%\FaxDesk). Your provider API token is stored there too, sealed with Windows data protection for your Windows account. Retention is a number of days you set; FaxDesk deletes older faxes on that schedule and keeps a daily backup in the same folder. If you turn on the whole-office option, other PCs on your own office network read that same folder through FaxDesk; nothing is copied to the internet.

Your fax provider has its own copy. Faxes pass through your provider (for example Phone.com) under their terms and, for healthcare offices, under the Business Associate Agreement between you and them. FaxDesk does not delete anything at the provider.

We cannot see your faxes. HnS, LLC has no access to any FaxDesk installation, its files or its provider account. If you email us for support, do not send us faxes, patient information or your API token; we will ask you to remove them.

The website (freefaxdesk.com)

The site is static pages hosted on GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare. We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages are read; it sets cookies in your browser and Google processes that data under its own terms. You can block it with any content blocker and the site works the same. Downloads come from GitHub, which has its own privacy policy. The forms on this site (reviews, Founding Offices, "tell us your fax service") are handled by a small program we run on Cloudflare; what you type is stored there, emailed to help@freefaxdesk.com, and used only for what the form says. If you tick the email box, your address goes to MailerLite, which sends you a confirmation first and puts an unsubscribe link in every email. A review is published only if you ticked that box and only with your first name, role and office type. Email help@freefaxdesk.com to have any of it deleted. The demo video on the home page is embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode, which sets no cookies until you press play. If you email help@freefaxdesk.com we keep the email to answer it. The "Buy us a coffee" link goes to a third-party site with its own policy.

Licence keys

A whole-office licence key is a code derived from the email address you give us when you buy it. We keep that email address and the date so we can re-issue the key if you lose it. Nothing else is collected and the key is never checked online.

Children

The software and site are for businesses and are not directed at children under 13.
